READ BEFORE GENERALIZING

The boundaries travel with the numbers.

  • One episode and two seed schedules are limited cases, not population evidence.
  • Future observed poses are used in the motion conditions; the study does not show control-only open-loop accuracy.
  • The standard norm triangle and the conditional correlation bound are derived statements, not original results.
  • Shared teacher forcing and algebraic identity closure do not validate sample pairing or the proposed correction mechanism.
  • The synthetic scalar controller is separate from the Cosmos data and says nothing about model or physical safety.
